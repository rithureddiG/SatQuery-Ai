# Change Detection

A valid change mission binds verified T1 and T2 observations, validates same-AOI coverage and compatible product/resolution, registers and normalizes the rasters, executes ChangeNet or an explicitly labeled deterministic raster-difference method, then produces probability, binary mask, magnitude, vectors, statistics, and evidence.

The current deterministic fallback is useful for contract testing and measured raster differences but is not a trained ChangeNet claim or a published benchmark score. Real LEVIR-CD training, checkpoint hashing, blind evaluation, and metrics remain pending.
